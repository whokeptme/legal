# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-05-25**

This Privacy Policy describes how the operator of the @whokeptme accounts
("we," "us") handles information in connection with the automated content
publishing system used to manage these accounts.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to the internal tooling used to publish content to the
@whokeptme accounts on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. The tooling is operated
by a single individual for personal use and is not offered as a service to
other users.

## 2. Information We Process

We process only the minimum information necessary to operate the publishing
system:

- **OAuth access and refresh tokens** issued by YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram
  to the @whokeptme accounts. These tokens authorize the tooling to publish
  content to the account owner's own accounts.
- **Account identifiers** (numeric user IDs, open IDs) returned by the
  respective platforms.
- **Publishing metadata** generated by the tooling itself: video titles,
  descriptions, hashtags, scheduled publish times, and upload status.

We do **not** collect, store, or process information about viewers, followers,
commenters, or any third parties. We do not run analytics on third-party users.

## 3. How Information Is Used

OAuth tokens and account identifiers are used solely to authenticate API calls
that publish content to the account owner's own accounts. They are not shared
with any third party.

## 4. Storage and Retention

All credentials and metadata are stored locally on infrastructure controlled
by the operator. Tokens are retained until they are revoked or expire.
Publishing metadata is retained indefinitely for operational and analytical
purposes.

## 5. Third Parties

We do not sell, lease, or share collected information with any third party.
API calls are made directly to YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram under their
respective terms.

## 6. Security

Credentials are stored with restricted file permissions on infrastructure
under the operator's sole control. Tokens are never transmitted to
unauthorized parties.

## 7. Children

The publishing system is not directed to children. The content distributed
through @whokeptme accounts is age-restricted where appropriate under each
platform's policies.

## 8. AI-Assisted Content Disclosure

Content distributed via @whokeptme accounts is produced with AI assistance
and is labeled as such using each platform's synthetic-content disclosure
mechanisms (YouTube's altered-content flag, TikTok's `is_aigc` flag, and
Instagram's caption-level disclosure).

## 9. Your Rights

If you believe the @whokeptme accounts have published content that involves
you and wish to request removal, contact us at the email below. We will
review the request and respond within a reasonable timeframe.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted at this URL with a
revised "Last updated" date.
